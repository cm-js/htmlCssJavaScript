Electronic Catalogue

“Electronic music is really popular among my generation since it's a genre with limitless possibilities and a lot of innovation, so I think people are interested in how much variety there is.” — YPulse

Subgenre-ception

Electronic music has probably the most subgenres compared to any other type of music. To know the difference between all of them is already difficult, but to organise those into a somewhat comprehensive and non-messy list is practicially impossible. Despite that, below is our best efforts to create such a list.

For a genre or subgenre to appear on this list, it has to be made primarily with electronic elements or electronic technology. This list may not be a complete one.
